About

Xin chào, hello! I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, advised by Prof. Yuzhang Shang. I graduated from Michigan State University with three majors and a 3.97 / 4.0 GPA, where I worked with Prof. Yu Kong and Yifan Li on embodied question answering in industrial scenes (IndustryEQA, NeurIPS 2025) and instruction-tuned multimodal models (FABA, ECCV 2024).

My research makes embodied AI efficient enough to run on real robots, across vision-language navigation, world action models and humanoids (see the roadmap). I'm open to collaborations and 2027 research internships, so feel free to email me.

LoopedWAM: Looped World Action Models for Elastic Inference

Anh Dao, Xinxin Liu, Hang Yu, Jeffrey A. Chan Santiago, Yuzhang Shang

arXiv 2026

GlanceNav: Efficient Vision-and-Language Navigation with Streaming Memory and Counterfactual Rewind

Anh Dao, Quan-Dung Pham, Danh Vinh Le, Tuyen Pham Le, Quan Nguyen, Yuzhang Shang

Under review
